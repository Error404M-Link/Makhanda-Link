About M-Link

Makhanda Link (M-LINK) is a web-based municipal information and service delivery platform designed to strengthen communication between the citizens of Makhanda and the Makana Local Municipality. The platform provides a central space where residents can access important community information, stay informed about minicipal notices and events, and events, and report municipal service delivery problems across wards.

M-Link brings communities, ward councillors and municipal officials together through a single digital platform. Residents can report faults relating to services such as water, sanitation, electricity, roads, parks and recreation, while ward councillors can manage, track and update these reports to support more effective service delivery.

Connecting communities through technology.

Our goal is to make communication between residents and the municipality faster, easier and more transparent.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a connected, informed and empowered Makhanda community where residents can communicate with their municipality and actively participate in improving the Makana community.

M-Link aims to support better service delivery by creating a transparent communication platform between communities, ward councillors and municipal officials.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide residents of Makhanda with a reliable and user-friendly digital platform for reporting municipal faults, accessing important community information and communicating with their local municipality.

Through technology, M-Link seeks to improve transparency, encourage community participation and help municipalities respond more effectively to service delivery challenges.

What M-Link Offers

M-Link provides residents and communities with digital tools that make it easier to communicate, report problems, access information and stay connected with their municipality.

Report Faults

Residents can report municipal service problems affecting their wards, including water, sanitation, electricity, roads, parks and recreation.

Track Progress

Residents and ward councillors can monitor the progress of reported faults and see when issues move from open to reported and eventually resolved.

Community Information

Residents can access important town-wide and ward-level information, including municipal notices, events, safety information and other community updates.

Ward Communication

Dedicated ward spaces allow residents and ward councillors to share information and focus on issues affecting their specific communities.

Transparency

M-Link supports greater transparency by making ward-level summaries and relevant municipal information available to the public.
